image: 36 verified stories across 8 regions (Europe 10, Middle East 6,
North America 5, Asia-Pacific 4, Oceania 3, Africa 3, South America 1,
Global 4), every story sourced from Reuters / AP / AFP and named
outlets, with a hero panel for the Kuwait drone strike, four large
panels, twelve picture tiles, a UTC timeline, CLAIM/CONFIRMED flags
and a date-check panel. Drawn as a dark Friday-night edition: deep
ink-navy ground, gold rules, red lead. Research was redone from
scratch and every story re-verified from its own primary source; the
date-check box names the two traps (Homs bombing 26 Dec 2025, Nepal
chopper crash 3 Nov 2026).
